// reconstruction pipeline

Three methods,
one goal.

We benchmark classical, optimization, and deep learning approaches — so you can choose the right trade-off between speed, interpretability, and quality.

🔬

Filtered Back Projection

The gold-standard baseline. Applies the inverse Radon transform via frequency-domain filtering. Fast and interpretable — but produces noisy images at low dose.

Classical · Signal Processing
∇

Total Variation Regularization

Treats reconstruction as an optimization problem. Solves y = Ax + ε with a TV prior that favors piecewise-smooth images while preserving sharp edges.

Optimization · Math-Based
⚡

Learned Primal-Dual

Unrolls the Primal-Dual Hybrid Gradient algorithm into a neural network. Combines physics-based forward modeling with learned CNN updates — outperforming TV by +4 dB PSNR.

Deep Learning · Physics-Aware
